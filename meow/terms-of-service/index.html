2. Description of Service

Meow is a privacy-focused Telegram client that encrypts messages, photos, videos, and documents using application-level encryption (AES-256) with a user-defined shared encryption key before transmission to Telegram’s servers. Meow operates on a single-chat configuration and does not support multi-chat functionality.

The App is provided for personal communication purposes between authorized users only. Meow does not host, store, or manage encrypted content — Telegram does. Meow merely provides an encryption layer on top of Telegram’s infrastructure.
